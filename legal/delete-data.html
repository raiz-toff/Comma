Data Deletion Request

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from Comma (developer: Rajkumar), and what is deleted or kept.

How to delete your data

Comma stores your data on your device, so you control it directly. You can delete it at any time in any of these ways.

1. Delete all data inside the app

Open Comma → Settings → Reset App. This permanently erases all of your data from the device, including:

  • Shifts (times, earnings, tips, GPS routes)
  • Expenses and receipt photos
  • Vehicles and odometer and maintenance history
  • Profile (name, country, currency)
  • Goals
  • Tax history

This cannot be undone.

2. Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Comma removes its private database and all data stored on your device.

3. Delete your Google Drive copy (if you turned on sync)

If you turned on Google Drive sync, encrypted files are stored in Comma's hidden appDataFolder in your own Google Drive. To delete them:

  • In Comma, reset cloud sync from Settings → Backup, and/or
  • Revoke Comma's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access and uninstalling the app deletes the folder's contents.

4. Ask by email

If you need help, email valium-banjo-badly@duck.com with the subject "Data deletion request".

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all the data listed above, on your device (through Reset App or uninstalling) and in Google Drive (through the steps above). Comma has no server, so the developer holds no other copy.
  • Kept: nothing. Comma runs no backend, collects no analytics, and stores no data on its own servers.
  • Retention period: data is removed as soon as you complete the steps above.
